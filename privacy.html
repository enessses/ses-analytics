Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

SES Analytics is a personal application used to analyze the performance of the owner's TikTok content.

The application may access information provided by TikTok through authorized permissions, including account information and statistics related to the owner's TikTok videos.

The information is used solely for personal content analysis, performance evaluation, and improving future content.

The application does not sell personal information or use TikTok data for advertising.

Data may be processed through third-party services required to provide the application, including TikTok and Composio. Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The application only accesses information after the user has granted the required authorization. Access can be revoked at any time through the user's TikTok account settings.

The application does not intentionally collect information from people who are not authorized users of the application.

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the application owner through the contact information associated with the application.
